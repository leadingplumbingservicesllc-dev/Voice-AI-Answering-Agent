# Example (fictional)

```text
LEADING PLUMBING - URGENT
Maria | +15555550142
West Palm Beach | Residential
Water leaking below kitchen sink. Active leak reported.
Callback: As soon as possible
Oct 23, 2026, 12:30 PM ET
Details: https://YOUR-DESK.example/?call=CALL_REFERENCE
```

The link requires an owner login. The alert uses city rather than street address. Unknown and unconfirmed information stays labeled. A deterministic formatter generates alerts even if the model fails. Callback digits and urgency are never removed to fit a segment. Actual messages can exceed two segments, especially with Unicode, long origins, names or descriptions. The inbox displays actual GSM-7/UCS-2 estimates per outbox row, including extension characters and multipart boundaries (160/153 septets or 70/67 UTF-16 units). Delivered is only a provider-confirmed state in live mode.
